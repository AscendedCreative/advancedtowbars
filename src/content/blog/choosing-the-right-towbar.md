Towbars come in many shapes and sizes—**light-duty**, **heavy-duty**, **fixed**, and **removable**—and choosing the right one depends on what you plan to tow.

Consider your vehicle's towing capacity, the type of trailer you’ll be pulling, and any future upgrades or accessories. For occasional use, a standard Class 2 towbar may be enough. For caravans, boats, or work trailers, a heavy-duty Class 4 towbar is more appropriate.

Don’t forget about compatibility with wiring kits, accessories like weight distribution hitches, and your vehicle’s make and model. When in doubt, consult an expert installer who understands local regulations and real-world towing demands.

A properly matched towbar is essential for safety, performance, and peace of mind.
