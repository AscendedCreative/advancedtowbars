When you're towing heavy loads, traditional braking systems aren't always enough. That’s where **electric brake controllers** come in. These devices synchronize your trailer’s brakes with your vehicle, improving safety and reducing wear on your vehicle's braking system.

Electric brakes offer better stopping power, smoother braking transitions, and allow for greater control—especially on long descents or in wet conditions. They're essential for trailers over 750kg gross trailer mass in Australia.

Not only do they keep you and your load safer, but they’re also legally required for many trailers. If you’re planning on towing anything serious, electric brakes are a must.
