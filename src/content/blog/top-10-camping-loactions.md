Australia is a camper’s paradise, offering some of the world’s most diverse and breathtaking landscapes. Whether you're towing a caravan, setting up a swag, or pulling in with your camper trailer, these 10 camping destinations promise unforgettable views, experiences, and serenity.

### 1. **Wilson’s Promontory, VIC**

Known as "The Prom," this national park offers pristine beaches, rainforests, and wildlife. Tidal River Campground is a great base with access to hikes and ocean views.

### 2. **Cape Range National Park, WA**

Camp next to the turquoise waters of Ningaloo Reef. Perfect for snorkeling, kayaking, and spotting whale sharks.

### 3. **Jervis Bay, NSW**

Famous for its powder-white sand and crystal-clear water. Green Patch and Cave Beach are ideal for both beach lovers and bush campers.

### 4. **Cradle Mountain–Lake St Clair, TAS**

Immerse yourself in alpine wilderness. Designated camping areas near Dove Lake offer dramatic views and access to epic hiking trails.

### 5. **Karijini National Park, WA**

Camp beneath ancient red rock gorges. Explore waterfalls, swimming holes, and adventure-filled hikes through Western Australia's outback.

### 6. **Fraser Island (K'gari), QLD**

The world’s largest sand island offers 4WD-access-only campsites next to freshwater lakes, beaches, and rainforest tracks.

### 7. **Blue Mountains, NSW**

Stay close to the cliffs and valleys that make this UNESCO-listed area famous. Blackheath and Euroka offer well-equipped campgrounds.

### 8. **Grampians National Park, VIC**

Rugged mountain ranges, Aboriginal rock art, and abundant wildlife await. Halls Gap is a popular spot with nearby camping facilities.

### 9. **Cape Tribulation, QLD**

Where the Daintree Rainforest meets the Great Barrier Reef. Eco-camping options offer a front-row seat to nature’s wonders.

### 10. **Flinders Ranges, SA**

Experience the red outback landscape and rich Indigenous heritage. Wilpena Pound Campground is a gateway to incredible bushwalks.

---

Each of these locations offers something unique—whether it’s stargazing under the outback sky or falling asleep to the sound of waves. Before you go, be sure to check local weather, permits, and towing requirements. Happy camping!
