Towbar Installation

Precision fitting, safety, and convenience for every vehicle

Factory Coded Wiring Solutions

Using factory coded wiring solutions and the latest software, you benefit from integrated safety features like:

  • Stability control
  • Reverse camera
  • City braking
  • Lane change warning
  • Seatbelt pre-tensioning systems
  • Reverse sensors
  • Electric rear tail gate
  • Active rear suspension systems

These systems adjust or deactivate automatically during towing for optimal performance.

Why Choose Us?

Our certified fitters provide professional on-site installation, tailored to your vehicle make and model. We're one of the few providers in Victoria offering both product and fitting in a single visit. Our lifetime warranty and fast turnaround times ensure peace of mind and convenience.
